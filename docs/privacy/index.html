Past Papers

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kinetix Engineering Solutions (“we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use the Past Papers mobile application and its related services.

Past Papers can be used as a guest without creating an account. Account features such as bookmarks, synchronised progress and discussions are optional.

1. Information we collect

Guest usage

Guest learners can browse subjects, topics, questions and memoranda without providing a name or email address.

Our infrastructure providers may process limited technical information needed to deliver and secure the service, including IP address, request time, requested resource, device or browser information, and error or security logs.

Account information

If an account is created, we process:

  • Email address and authentication identifier.
  • Whether the account is managed by an adult learner or by a parent or guardian for an under-18 learner.
  • The version and date of the relevant account declaration.
  • Optional learner display name and school grade.

For a guardian-managed learner account, the parent or guardian must use their own email address. We encourage learners to use a suitable display name rather than disclosing an unnecessary full legal name.

Study activity

Signed-in account holders may generate:

  • Question bookmarks and saved questions.
  • Question and topic study progress.
  • Reflection or “Needs review” selections.
  • Community Guidelines acceptance records.

Community information

If discussion features are used, we process:

  • Discussion comments.
  • Optional YouTube or TikTok links added to comments.
  • Comment reports and report reasons.
  • Blocked-learner relationships.
  • Moderation status and related records.

A comment’s display name, text, supported external link and posting time may be visible to other users. Account email addresses are not displayed publicly.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Create, authenticate and secure accounts.
  • Synchronise bookmarks and study progress.
  • Provide learner profiles and account features.
  • Operate question discussions.
  • Investigate reports, prevent abuse and enforce Community Guidelines.
  • Respond to support and deletion requests.
  • Diagnose faults and protect the application, users and infrastructure.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

3. Children and guardian-managed accounts

Past Papers is intended for learners aged 13 and older, including learners under 18.

An account for a learner under 18 must be created and managed by their parent or legal guardian. The adult must use their own email address and confirm that they consent to creating and managing the learner account.

Learners can continue using the core study experience as guests without creating an account.

A parent or guardian may contact us to review, correct, withdraw consent for, or delete information connected to a guardian-managed account.

4. Service providers and international processing

We use service providers to operate the application, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, PostgreSQL data storage, server functions and question-image storage.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers used to operate the Past Papers Content API and public website.
  • GitHub Pages for public policy and account-deletion pages.

These providers may process information in countries outside South Africa. We limit the information provided to what is reasonably necessary for them to supply their services and protect the application.

5. External links

Discussion comments may contain links to YouTube or TikTok. Selecting an external link takes you to a third-party service governed by that service’s own privacy policy and terms. We do not control how those external services process information.

6. Advertising

Advertising is currently disabled in Past Papers. We do not currently use advertising data to personalise or serve advertisements.

If advertising is enabled in the future, we will implement the required consent and age-appropriate safeguards and update this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosures before activation.

7. Retention and deletion

Account information is generally retained while an account remains active or for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service.

An account can be permanently deleted inside the app or through our public account-deletion page .

Account deletion removes the account, profile, bookmarks, progress, comments, Community Guidelines acceptances, submitted reports and block relationships linked to that account.

A moderation record concerning another learner’s report may be retained in anonymised form after deletion and will no longer reference the deleted account. Limited security logs or backup copies may also remain temporarily where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or infrastructure recovery.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including authenticated access, database access controls, row-level security, protected server functions and encrypted network connections.

No online system is completely secure. Account holders should use a strong password and should not share their credentials.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request:

  • Access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of your account and associated information.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing relies on it.
  • Information about how your personal information is used.

Certain profile information can be corrected directly inside the application.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.
