Past Papers

Delete your learner account

Past Papers is provided by Kinetix Engineering Solutions. You can permanently delete your account either inside the mobile app or by sending us a deletion request.

Delete your account inside the app

  1. Sign in to Past Papers.
  2. Open the Account screen.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.
  5. Select Delete permanently.

In-app deletion is processed immediately.

Request deletion outside the app

Send the request from the same email address associated with your Past Papers account. Use the subject Past Papers account deletion request.

Email a deletion request

Requests are normally processed within 7 days. We may contact you using your account email if ownership needs to be verified.

Data that is deleted

Deleting your account removes data linked to it, including:

  • Your learner profile and account credentials.
  • Saved questions and bookmarks.
  • Question and topic study progress.
  • Your discussion comments.
  • Community Guidelines acceptances.
  • Blocked-learner relationships.
  • Reports submitted from your account.

Data that may be retained

Moderation records concerning another learner's report may be retained in anonymised form after deletion. They will no longer contain a reference to your deleted account.

Limited security logs or backup copies may also remain temporarily where required for system security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or infrastructure recovery.
